Part 3: Which choices to use and when

Let’s go *choice first.

*label TheCrossroad
You stand at a crossroad.

*choice
    # Go left.
        The path is clear and you see a lake nearby.
        *goto LeftPath
    # Go right.
        The path is clear but there are strange noises and an even stranger smell.
        *goto RightPath

*label LeftPath
You walk down the left path and see deer drinking from the lake.

*label RightPath
You walk down the right path and see vultures eating the carcass of a horse.

What is *label?

*label marks a position in your scene and is used for branching paths. You don't have to label every scene, only if you want the player to be able to return to a scene later, jump forward to a scene or for branching paths.

When you want to jump to a specific scene, you use:

*goto (Whatever you called the scene) - Spaces are not allowed in the label name, it'll only give you an error. See above, its *label LeftPath and not *label Left Path.

This lets you move around your story.

Now for *fake_choice

*fake_choice is used for flavor text. Use it when you want all choices lead to the same scene. Usually the only difference is a brief reaction or flavour text to make the choice seem meaningful and not pointless.

*label TheCrossroad
You stand at a crossroad.

*fake_choice
    #Check the left path.
        The path is clear and you see a lake nearby but your unsure this is the right way.
        *goto TurnAround
    #Check the right path.
        The path is clear but there's strange noises and an even stranger smell. You hope that's not the path you have to take.
        *goto TurnAround

*label TurnAround
You stand there, frozen from your indecisiveness. Unsure which path to take because you lost the map. Truly pathetic.

The above example uses *goto but you can also leave it out since both choices go to the same scene. However *choice must have a *goto at the end of each choice, otherwise you'll get an error.

When to use *choice vs *fake_choice

So for a quick recap, use *choice when the path truly branches (different scenes, different stats, etc.).

And use *fake_choice when the options are only for flavor and all lead to the same place/scene.
