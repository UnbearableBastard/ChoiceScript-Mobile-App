Part 5: The Stats Screen

Every ChoiceScript game has a stats screen that the players can open at any time.

The stats screen is managed by choicescript_stats.txt. This file is automatically created by the app just like the startup.txt file and cannot be deleted or renamed.

What goes in choicescript_stats.txt?

The most common command is *stat_chart.

*stat_chart
  text name
  percent strength
  percent health

When the player opens the stats screen, they might see something like:

Name: Peter

Strength: [██████░░░░] 60%

Health: [████████░░] 80%

*stat_chart

*stat_chart tells ChoiceScript that you're' about to list some stats to display. Each line under it must be indented at least once.

There are several ways to show a stat:

1. text stats

Use text for words, names, or short descriptions.

*stat_chart
    Name: $!{firstname}
    Title: $!{title}

This will show the current values of the variables firstname and title when you use ${} (for example, “Chris” and “The Gobshite").

NoteIf a sentence starts with the players name or pronouns add a ! after the $ to capitalize the first letter. Like this: $!{}.

2. percent stats

Use percent for numbers from 0 to 100. ChoiceScript shows them as a bar plus the number.

*stat_chart
  percent strength
  percent health
  percent gold

Make sure the variables are numeric (for example: *create strength 50 in startup.txt).

3. Opposed pairs

Opposed pairs are stats that pull in opposite directions, like “Kind / Cruel” or “Lawful / Chaotic”.

In your stats screen you can show them like this:

*stat_chart
  opposed_pair lawful
    chaotic
  opposed_pair kind
    cruel

Updating stats during the story

You can change variables inside your scenes using *set. The stats screen will reflect any changes and show their current values.

For example, in startup.txt:

*create name "Liam"
*create strength 50
*create lawful 50

During the story:

You help the lost sheep find their way home.

*set kind +10
*set lawful +5

Then later, when the player checks the stats screen, the bar for kindness and lawfulness will be updated with the new values.

Showing custom text on the stats screen

You can also include normal story-style text and conditions in choicescript_stats.txt.

*if strength >= 60
  You are impressively strong.
*elseif strength >= 40
  You are average in strength.
*else
  You are weak and pathetic.

Summary

choicescript_stats.txt controls what the player sees on the stats screen.

*stat_chart shows variables as text, percent bars, or opposed pairs.

You change stats in your story scenes with *set

You can use *if, *elseif, and *else to show different descriptions based on the player’s stats.
