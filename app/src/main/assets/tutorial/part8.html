Part 8: Common Errors & Debugging

You're going to break your game (probably a lot). Don't worry, that's normal. Here are some of the most common errors and how to fix them.

“Non-existent variable”

This usually means you forgot to *create a variable in startup.txt.

If you see this error, check for spelling differences and make sure the *create line exists.

“Invalid indent” or “It is illegal to fall out of a *choice”

These errors often come from indentation problems, mostly from *choice, *fake_choice and # options.

The # lines are indented under *choice.

The text after each # is indented one more level.

The variable *set is on the same indent as the text

Remember to use two or four indents. Not both.

A extra indent or outdent can confuse the parser.

“No label <name>” or “Could not find label”

When you use *goto lost_label, there must be a matching *label lost_label in the same scene file.

If you see this error, check the spelling of the label and the *goto.

There is an error checker included within the editor. Highlight the area and hit run, you'll either get no errors or a message detailing the current errors.
