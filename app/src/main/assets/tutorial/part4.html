Part 4: Stats & Conditions

Creating variables

In startup.txt, you'll create and name your variables with *create:

*create name "Thomas"
*create strength 8
*create gold 230
  • name is text.
  • strength and gold are numbers.
  • You can also leave them blank and set them later. So 'gold 0' or 'name ""'

Changing variables with *set

When you want to increase or decrease or change said variable or stat, you'll use *set.

*set gold +5
*set strength -1
*set name "Riley"

When you use +5, that adds 5 gold, if you use -5, that removes 5 gold, and if you just put a flat number like 5, the gold amount becomes 5. This goes for every stat.

Using *if and *else

*if strength > 5
    You easily lift the heavy door.
*else
    The door will not budge.

You can also combine conditions:

*if (strength > 5) and (gold >= 10)
    You pay the guard and push the door open.

If you want to check for one or the other condition, switch the and with or

*if (strength > 5) or (gold >= 10)
    You push the door open.

Conditional choices

Sometimes a choice should only appear when a condition is met.

*choice
    *if (gold >= 5)
        # Buy a drink.
            You hand over a few coins.
    # Leave the tavern.
        You step back into the night.

The choice to buy a drink only shows up if the player has at least 5 gold. You could also show the choice but make it unselectable if the condition isn't met.

*choice
    *selectableif (gold >= 5) # Buy a drink.
        You hand over a few coins.
    #Leave the tavern.
        You step back into the night.
