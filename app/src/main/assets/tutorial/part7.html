Part 7: Temporary Variables & *rand

*create vs *temp

So *create creates permanent variable that last for the whole game and can be modified in any scene file.

*create strength 5
*create gold 0

But sometimes you'll just want a temporary variable for one scene/chapter. That's where *temp comes in:

*temp roll 0
*rand roll 1 6
You rolled a ${roll}.

*temp variables exist only in the current scene.

They are great for one-off calculations.

*rand: random numbers

*rand gives you a random number between two values:

*temp roll 0
*rand roll 1 6
You rolled a ${roll}.

You can use this for dice, chance events, or skill checks.

Simple skill check example

*create strength 5

*temp roll 0
*rand roll 1 10

You push against the boulder...

*if (roll + strength) >= 12
  The boulder rolls aside. You made it!
*else
  It does not move. Maybe next time.

Random events with choices

You can also randomize what happens after a choice:

*choice
    # Search the room.
        *temp find 0
        *rand find 1 3
        *if find = 1
            You find a healing potion.
            *set potions +1
        *elseif find = 2
            You find a few coins.
            *set gold +5
        *else
            You find nothing of interest.

Be cautious about putting *rand on the same page as the *if.

If the player doesn't get a good roll, they can go into the stats screen and back out to reroll.

To prevent this, use *rand on the previous page (before the player pressed the "next" button or within a choice like above).
